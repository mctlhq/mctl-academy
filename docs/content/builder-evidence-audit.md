# Builder evidence audit

Date: 2026-09-05. Scope: all 80 previously published Agentic AI Builder questions.
Agent inspection of each stem, designated correct answer and supplied excerpt;
retained items' options/explanations were also inspected. This is a support audit,
not human publication approval or re-verification of private snapshot bytes.

## Outcome

- 28 retain existing publication; 52 are quarantined as needs_review.
- Quarantine is based on an individually identified support gap, not an automated
  source/objective or text-length threshold. Some answers may be factually correct
  but are not established by their supplied evidence.
- Existing question IDs, text, evidence, author/reviewer records and attempts are
  preserved. Prior reviewed metadata is historical, not approval of a rewrite.
- Before this audit, 59/80 correct options were strictly longer than every distractor.
  Duplicate concepts include Fast flavors, account signup, checkpoints and quota
  growth. Retained items still need future editorial improvements; those findings
  alone do not establish that an item is factually wrong.
- With 28 retained items the existing 30-question weighted Mock is unavailable:
  domain-2 has 7 of 10 required questions and domain-3 has 5 of 6. Keep the shortfall
  visible; do not silently change exam composition or republish unsupported items.

## Repair workflow

For each quarantined item, locate a supporting passage in an allowlisted, captured
source, narrow unsupported claims, and rewrite distractors from documented
misconceptions. Preserve its ID for the same concept; do not reuse IDs for unrelated
questions. Submit as review_ready and obtain human approval. Never change source
hashes or reviewed.by to manufacture evidence or approval.

Public source capture and human publication approval remain separate operations.
Bank expansion and Tavily allowlisting are follow-ups, not accomplished by this audit.
